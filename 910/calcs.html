1. How we approached it1. 考え方

  1. A Japanese timber house resists earthquakes and wind with braced walls. The south-east corner of the living room is glass on both faces, so there is no braced wall there. We use frames instead: a post and the beam over the glass, made stiff at the corner with knee braces (short diagonal struts), and we brace the walls that are solid.日本の木造住宅は耐力壁で地震や風に抵抗します。リビングの南東の角は両面ともガラスで耐力壁がないため、柱と窓上の梁を方杖（短い斜材）で固めた門型フレームとし、壁のある部分には筋交いを入れています。
  2. Kutchan has heavy snow (design depth 230 cm). In heavy-snow areas 35% of the snow counts in the earthquake weight, so the earthquake load (33.4 kN on the living wing's upper floor) is bigger than the wind load (18.2 kN): the earthquake governs.倶知安町は多雪区域（垂直積雪量230 cm）です。多雪区域では地震時の重量に積雪の35%を加えるため、リビング棟2階の地震力（33.4 kN）が風荷重（18.2 kN）を上回り、地震で決まります。
  3. The wing's load goes to its wall lines: east–west half to the south line and half to the north line (16.7 kN each); north–south 56% to the east line (18.7 kN, from where the weight sits; the dining side has no 2F wall). On a line, the frames and the braced walls move together, so each takes load in proportion to its stiffness: a small frame model gives the frames' stiffness, and a braced wall reaches its allowed load at a sway of 1/120 (the Japanese wall-multiplier rule). Because a stiffer wall would take more, the east wall is also checked at 1/200.リビング棟の水平力は各通りが負担します。東西方向は南通りと北通りが半分ずつ（各 16.7 kN）、南北方向は東通りが56%（18.7 kN、重量の位置から。ダイニング側は2階に壁なし）。同じ通りの門型フレームと耐力壁は一緒に変形するため、剛性の比で分担します。フレームの剛性は簡単なフレームモデルで求め、耐力壁は層間変形角1/120で許容耐力に達するもの（壁倍率の考え方）としています。壁が硬いほど負担が増えるため、東面の耐力壁は1/200でも確認しています。
  4. Target: every member at most 60% of its allowed strength (the allowed strengths already include the code's safety margins). Result: at most 60% (the east braced wall in the stiffer case; 49% by the standard rule).目標：すべての部材で許容耐力の60%以下（許容耐力には安全率が含まれています）。結果：最大60%（東面の耐力壁を硬めに見た場合。標準の考え方では49%）。
  5. Windows: the wind pressure on the glass needs grade S-3. By the makers' own size tables, at full height and 1,690 wide only LIXIL's 2-panel sliding window (4 mm triple glass) and YKK AP's APW 430 fixed window (5 mm triple glass) reach S-3, and joining two windows is not rated that wide, so the big glass is single 1,690 units (fixed or sliding) with a post between them; the posts are checked for snow, floor and wind.窓：ガラスにかかる風圧から耐風圧S-3が必要です。メーカーのサイズ別性能表では、全高・幅1,690でS-3を満たすのはLIXILの2枚建引違い窓（ガラス 4-A-1.3-A-4）とYKK AP APW 430 のFIX窓（ガラス 5+A+5+A+5）で、連窓もその幅ではS-3を満たさないため、幅1,690の単体窓（FIXまたは引違い）を柱で区切って並べ、その柱を雪・床・風で検討しています。
