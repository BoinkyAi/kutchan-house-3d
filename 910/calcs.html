1. How we approached it1. 考え方

  1. A Japanese timber house resists earthquakes and wind with braced walls. The south-east corner of the living room is glass on both faces, so there is no braced wall there. We use frames instead: a post and the beam over the glass, made stiff at the corner with knee braces (short diagonal struts), and we brace the walls that are solid.日本の木造住宅は耐力壁で地震や風に抵抗します。リビングの南東の角は両面ともガラスで耐力壁がないため、柱と窓上の梁を方杖（短い斜材）で固めた門型フレームとし、壁のある部分には筋交いを入れています。
  2. Kutchan has heavy snow (design depth 230 cm). In heavy-snow areas 35% of the snow counts in the earthquake weight, so the earthquake load (32.0 kN on the living wing's upper floor) is bigger than the wind load (18.2 kN): the earthquake governs.倶知安町は多雪区域（垂直積雪量230 cm）です。多雪区域では地震時の重量に積雪の35%を加えるため、リビング棟2階の地震力（32.0 kN）が風荷重（18.2 kN）を上回り、地震で決まります。
  3. Each wall line of the wing (south, east, north) takes half of that load: 16.0 kN. On a line, the frames and braced walls share it in proportion to their strength (they reach their limits together; Japanese wall quantities are added up the same way).リビング棟の各通り（南・東・北）がその半分 16.0 kN を負担します。同じ通りの門型フレームと耐力壁は耐力の比で分担します（壁量計算と同じく耐力を足し合わせる考え方）。
  4. Target: every member at most 60% of its allowed strength (the allowed strengths already include the code's safety margins). Result: at most 53%.目標：すべての部材で許容耐力の60%以下（許容耐力には安全率が含まれています）。結果：最大53%。
  5. Windows: the wind pressure on the glass needs grade S-3. By LIXIL's own size tables the widest window that reaches S-3 at full height is the 1,690-wide 2-panel sliding window (with 4 mm triple glass), and joining two windows is not rated that wide, so the big glass is single 1,690 sliding windows with a post between them; the posts are checked for snow, floor and wind.窓：ガラスにかかる風圧から耐風圧S-3が必要です。LIXILのサイズ別性能表では、全高でS-3を満たす最も幅の広い窓は幅1,690の2枚建引違い窓（ガラス 4-A-1.3-A-4）で、連窓もその幅ではS-3を満たさないため、幅1,690の引違い窓を柱で区切って並べ、その柱を雪・床・風で検討しています。

告示1458 method: W = q̂ × Ĉf,  q̂ = 0.6 Er² V0²,  Er = 1.7 (H/ZG)^α  (H = average of the height 8.966 m and the eave 6.805 m = 7.89 m, terrain III: ZG 450, α 0.20)
q̂ = 0.6 × 0.757² × 34² = 398 N/m²
pushing in, 2F glass (taken at z = 8 m):  Ĉpe = Cpe × Gpe = 1.006 × 3.031, minus the inside −0.5 → W = 1,411 Pa

suction:  −1.8 (general) / −2.2 (corner zones) → 716 / 875 Pa
→ every window needs wind grade S-3 (1,600 Pa); S-2 (1,200 Pa) is not enough on either floor.

Legal note: the 告示1458 check is required by law only where a structural calculation is (for a timber house since April 2025: 3 or more storeys, or over 300 m²); this house (2 storeys, 253 m²) is below that, so we use it as our design basis for picking the window grade.法的には、告示1458の検討は構造計算が必要な建築物（木造は2025年4月以降、3階建て以上または延べ面積300 m²超）が対象です。本計画（2階建て・253 m²）は対象外のため、窓の等級を決める設計の根拠として用いています。
