How to move

Keys — WASD walk forward / left / back / right · E/Q up / down a floor (in Fly: rise / sink) · Shift run
Pan the view — ←→↑↓ (or JLIK) look left / right / up / down
Lens — Z wider · X narrower · mouse wheel / pinch too. The angle is across the screen's long side (72° ≈ a 24 mm lens); walls stay upright until you look more than 24° up or down.
Mouse / touch — drag to pan the view (the picture follows your hand). Mouse look = FPS-style aiming (Esc to release).
Shortcuts — 123 jump to a floor · 0 outside · F walk / fly · O orbit · C roof · T labels · G quality

Walk keeps you at eye height on the floor you are on and stops you at walls. Fly goes anywhere, through everything.
The dropdown jumps to any room. Roof off + Floors (cut-away) + Fly = doll-house view.

Geometry is the real plan (Floor Plan Creator export): 3 floors, 2.74 m ceilings, 37 cm exterior walls, double-height gym, bath, entrance and living, every window and stair as drawn. Finishes are indicative, not specified. Textures, sky and furniture models: Poly Haven (CC0).
