What changed

  • Every window now uses standard YKK AP sizes. The big openings are split into 2.6 m and 1.69 m bays: each bay is a sliding door from the floor with a 0.77 m fixed window stacked above, up to 3.39 m. The 20 windows on the drawing become 28 bays of 52 real units.
  • The entrance is a double door with two equal leaves, 1.69 × 2.33 m (was 1.5 × 2.0 m).
  • The view (south) side keeps 20.7 m of windows along it (24.1 m as drawn). The ground-floor windows now start at the floor.
  • Bedroom 1: its west window no longer runs into the walk-in closet, and its door from the hall opens the full 1.0 m.

What changed

  • Windows: every pane is now a standard YKK triple-glazed unit. There are four 2.6 m sliding glass doors on the view side on each floor, lined up, with fixed transoms over the two in the living room. All other windows are standard sizes, and bathrooms have frosted glass.
  • Sauna 3.3 → 5.0 m² (2.7 × 1.8 m, L-shaped benches). It pushes into the bath and still opens into it; the bath goes from 22.8 to 21.1 m².
  • Loft: the storage room is now a bathroom (shower unit, toilet, washstand; 5.0 m²) plus a 2.5 m² closet for the loft bedroom.
  • Front door: a double door (two leaves, 1.69 × 2.33 m) under the carport.
  • Roof unchanged: high on the east, low on the west, so snow slides off the west side, away from the door, carport and view glass.

Plan e · Plan B — Ichijo (house 7)

Open in editor3D

What changed

  • Every window is now one of Ichijo's stock sizes: floor-to-2.17 m sliding doors 1.65 or 2.49 m wide, spaced on Ichijo's 91 cm grid (no custom 2.2 m panes, no floating 30 cm transoms).
  • Living high wall redesigned: one or two 47 cm fixed rows sit right on top of the sliding doors on the side walls (same width, 15 cm wall between), a 1.9 m fixed pane over each on the tall east end wall, all within 6.4 m of the slope's low edge.
  • Solid wall at every outside corner (Ichijo needs about 0.9 m on one side), including the metre beside the tall end wall.
  • Front door: a two-leaf parent-child door, 1.24 × 2.33 m (was a 1.0 m single).
  • South (Mt Yotei) glass 48.8 m², 66 % (was 54.7 m² in non-standard sizes).

Plan f · Plan B — Ichijo · compact (house 8)

Open in editor3D

What changed

  • Roof rebuilt to Ichijo's rules: a flat parapet roof over the main block (6.39 m) and one 3.5-in-10 slope (Ichijo's only pitch) over the living wing only, rising east to 8.93 m (was one 2-in-10 slope over the whole house, 14.9 m long; Ichijo allows 8.2 m).
  • The slope is 6.64 m long (7.3 of Ichijo's 91 cm squares, max 9); its snow slides west onto the flat roof, none toward the carport or the road.
  • Living ceiling 3.20 → 4.75 m over 20.7 m² (Ichijo's maximum) with the same high wall as Plan B — Ichijo: sliding doors with fixed windows stacked on top.
  • Ichijo stock-size windows everywhere, solid outside corners, a parent-child front door 1.24 × 2.33 m.
  • Highest point 8.93 m (was 9.55 m); south glass 48.8 m², 66 %.
日本語
  • 屋根を一条の仕様どおりに：母屋はパラペット付きフラット屋根（6.39m）、リビング棟だけ一条唯一の3.5寸の片流れで東へ上がり最高8.93m（以前は家全体に2寸の片流れ・長さ14.9m、一条の上限は8.2m）。
  • 片流れの長さは6.64m（一条の91cmマスで7.3マス、上限9マス）。雪は西のフラット屋根に落ち、カーポート側・道路側には落ちない。
  • リビングの天井は3.20→4.75m・20.7㎡（一条の上限）、プランB 一条版と同じ高い壁：掃き出し窓の上にFIX窓を重ねる。
  • 窓はすべて一条の規格サイズ、外側の角は壁、玄関は親子ドア1.24×2.33m。
  • 最高高さ8.93m（以前9.55m）、南面のガラス48.8㎡・66%。
